# Commonly confused words

- affect (verb: to influence) / effect (noun: a result)
- complement (to complete) / compliment (to praise)
- comprise (the whole comprises the parts) / compose (the parts compose the whole)
- ensure (make certain) / insure (cover against loss) / assure (tell confidently)
- fewer (countable) / less (uncountable)
- its (possessive) / it's (it is)
- principal (main) / principle (rule)
- stationary (not moving) / stationery (paper)
- that (restrictive clause) / which (non-restrictive, after a comma)
