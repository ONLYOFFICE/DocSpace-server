# Contract review checklist

## Every agreement must state
- The full legal names and registration details of the parties
- The subject of the agreement and the deliverables
- The price, currency, payment schedule and taxes
- The term, renewal and termination conditions
- Liability limits and indemnities
- Confidentiality and personal data handling
- Governing law and dispute resolution
- Who may sign and how notices are sent

## Escalate to a lawyer when
- Liability is unlimited or the cap is above the standard
- The agreement grants exclusivity or a non-compete
- Intellectual property is assigned away from the company
- The term is longer than three years or renews automatically
