# Slide writing rules

- One message per slide; the title states it as a sentence.
- At most three supporting points, at most six words of body text per line.
- One chart or image per slide; the chart title says what it shows.
- Font size never below 24 pt for body text.
- Numbers are rounded and carry units and a period.
- The source of every number is in the footer.
- The last slide is the ask or the next step, never "Thank you".
