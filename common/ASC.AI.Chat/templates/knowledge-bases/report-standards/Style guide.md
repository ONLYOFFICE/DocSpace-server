# Report style guide

## Voice
- Active voice and present tense for findings.
- Say who does what: "Sales lost 4% of accounts", not "accounts were lost".

## Numbers
- Always give the period and the unit.
- Compare with the previous period or the plan, never a number alone.
- Round to the precision the decision needs.

## Words to avoid
- Significant, substantial, considerable: give the number instead.
- Going forward, at this point in time, in order to.

## Headings
- Sentence case, no full stop, at most eight words.
