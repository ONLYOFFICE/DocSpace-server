# Visual selection guide

- Change over time: line chart
- Comparison of categories: horizontal bar chart
- Share of a whole (up to five parts): stacked bar or pie
- Relationship between two measures: scatter plot
- Process or sequence: numbered steps
- Structure or hierarchy: tree diagram
- A single important number: large figure with one line of context
- Before and after: two columns

Avoid 3D charts, more than five colours and tables with more than four columns.
