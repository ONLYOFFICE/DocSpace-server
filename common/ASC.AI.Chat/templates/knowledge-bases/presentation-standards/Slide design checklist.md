# Slide design checklist

- Title is a sentence and fits on two lines
- No more than three points and thirty words of body text
- Body text is at least 24 pt
- One visual per slide, labelled directly, no legend when avoidable
- At most three colours plus grey; the accent colour marks the message
- Elements are aligned to a grid; margins are equal
- Slide number and source line are present
- No clip art, animations only where they explain a sequence
