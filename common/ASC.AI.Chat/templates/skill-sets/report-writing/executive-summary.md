# Executive summary

## Steps
1. Find the decision or question the document answers.
2. Extract at most five key points, each backed by one number or fact.
3. State the recommendation and what happens if nothing is done.

## Output
At most 250 words: one opening sentence with the conclusion, the key points as a list, the recommendation, the next step with an owner and a date when the source gives them.

## Rules
- No new information that is not in the source.
- No background the reader already knows.
