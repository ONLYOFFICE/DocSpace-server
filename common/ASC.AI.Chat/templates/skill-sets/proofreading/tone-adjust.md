# Tone adjust

## Steps
1. Name the current tone and the target tone or audience.
2. Rewrite paragraph by paragraph, keeping every fact, number and name.
3. Compare the result with the original and list anything that was dropped.

## Output
The rewritten text and a short note on what changed in tone.

## Rules
- The meaning and the order of ideas stay the same.
- A shorter version keeps all decisions, dates and amounts.
