# Key messages

## Steps
1. Identify the purpose of the document and its intended reader.
2. Collect every conclusion, decision and recommendation with its supporting number.
3. Rank them by how much they matter to the reader; keep five to seven.

## Output
A ranked list: Message, Evidence, Source section or page.

## Rules
- A message is a full sentence with a verb, not a topic.
- Never merge two conclusions that have different evidence.
