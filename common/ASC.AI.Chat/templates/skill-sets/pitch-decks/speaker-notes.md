# Speaker notes

## Steps
1. Read the deck and time budget; allow about one minute per slide unless told otherwise.
2. For each slide write what to say that is not on the slide: the transition in, the point, one example, the transition out.
3. Mark the slides where questions are likely and prepare a short answer.

## Output
Notes per slide, 60 to 90 words each, in spoken language.

## Rules
- Do not read the slide text back.
- Keep numbers the speaker says identical to those on the slide.
