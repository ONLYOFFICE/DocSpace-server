# Slide simplify

## Steps
1. State the one message the slide should carry.
2. Remove everything that does not support it; move detail to notes or a backup slide.
3. If two messages remain, split the slide.
4. Suggest a visual that can replace a list.

## Output
The simplified slide (title, up to three points, visual), plus what was moved and where.

## Rules
- No facts are deleted silently; moved content is listed.
- The simplified slide passes the design checklist.
