# Budget builder

## Steps
1. Agree the period, the currency and the level of detail.
2. Sort the items into the standard budget categories; mark each as fixed or variable.
3. Spread the amounts over the months: fixed evenly, variable by its driver or by last year's seasonality.
4. Add totals per month and per category and a list of the assumptions used.

## Output
A table with categories as rows and months as columns plus a Total column, followed by an Assumptions block.

## Rules
- Every amount traces back to an input or an assumption.
- Totals are formulas, never typed numbers.
