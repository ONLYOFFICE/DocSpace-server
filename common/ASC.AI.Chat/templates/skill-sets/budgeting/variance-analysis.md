# Variance analysis

## Steps
1. Align the plan and the actual by category and period.
2. Calculate the variance: actual minus plan, and as a percentage of plan.
3. Flag variances above the threshold in the budgeting method.
4. Look for the cause of each flagged variance: timing, volume, price or a one-off.

## Output
A table with Category, Plan, Actual, Variance, Variance %, Cause; then the three largest variances explained, and the forecast for the rest of the period if the trend holds.

## Rules
- Favourable and unfavourable variances are labelled, not left to the sign.
- A cause that the data does not show is marked as an assumption.
